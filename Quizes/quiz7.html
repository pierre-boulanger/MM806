Lighting & Shading Quiz

Section 5: Lighting and Shading Models

1
What are the three primary types of photoreceptors in the human eye and how do they contribute to color vision?
Human vision: 400-700 nanometer spectrum with rod and cone cells
2
What is the mathematical formula for the Lambertian (diffuse) lighting model and what does each component represent?
Rc = Lc × Oc × (On · -Ln)
3
What are the three components of the Phong lighting model and how do they contribute to realistic surface appearance?
Phong Model = Ambient + Diffuse + Specular components
4
What is the key advantage of Physically Based Shading (PBS) over traditional lighting models in modern real-time graphics?
PBS simulates real-world light-material interactions based on physics principles
5
What are shaders and how do they differ from the older fixed function pipeline in graphics rendering?
Modern GPUs use programmable shaders instead of fixed function operations
