What is the alpha blending formula used for transparency effects, and what do the variables represent?
R = As × Rs + (1 - As) × Rb
G = As × Gs + (1 - As) × Gb
B = As × Bs + (1 - As) × Bb
2
What is the Z-buffer (depth buffer) algorithm and what are its main advantages for real-time graphics?
For each pixel: if z(x,y) < z_buffer(x,y) then update color and depth
3
What is antialiasing and why is it important for computer graphics, especially in VR applications?
VR requires high-quality antialiasing to prevent visual artifacts that can cause discomfort
4
What are the key performance optimization challenges specific to VR and AR applications?
VR/AR requires 90+ FPS, low latency, and stereo rendering for comfortable user experience

What are some emerging trends and future directions in real-time graphics that will impact VR/AR development?
Future graphics: Real-time ray tracing, AI-assisted rendering, cloud streaming, advanced displays
