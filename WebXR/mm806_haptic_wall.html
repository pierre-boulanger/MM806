Haptic rendering of a virtual wall: spring + damper on the probe

The device position (HIP) penetrates the wall; the proxy stays on the surface; the spring and damper between them produce the force sent to the motors at the servo rate.
F = −K·d − B·ḋ  (d > 0)
F = 0  (d ≤ 0, free space)
Hand
Virtual wall
Device
Try 100 Hz with K = 5000 N/m: the force is held constant between samples, the wall returns more energy than it absorbs and the probe chatters. The sufficient passivity condition (Colgate & Schenkel) is b > K·T/2 + |B|, with T the servo period.
