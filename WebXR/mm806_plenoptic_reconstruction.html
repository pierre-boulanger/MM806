Digital refocusing

Each sub-aperture is a slightly different viewpoint. Shift every sub-aperture image by an amount set by the chosen focal plane and add them: whatever lies at that depth lines up and is sharp, while everything else averages into blur. Change the shift and you refocus — after the photo was taken.
