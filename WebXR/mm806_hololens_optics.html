OPTICSA microdisplay's collimated light is diffracted into the glass, bounces by total internal reflection, and is diffracted back out over the eye. Each pixel = one beam angle; the out-coupler replicates the pupil to make an eyebox. The glass stays transparent, so you also see the real world.
