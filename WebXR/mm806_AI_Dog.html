Point at the floor and tap to place Nova

Nova, an autonomous AI dog

Nova runs on a utility-based behaviour AI: internal drives (energy, need for attention, play drive, curiosity) and moods (happiness, excitement) compete every few hundred milliseconds to pick a behaviour. She perceives where you are, whether you are looking at her, whether you crouch, your hands, and the ball. Obedience is learned: praise within a few seconds of a command she obeyed strengthens it; praise or "no" during spontaneous behaviour makes it more or less likely in future.

Desktop / phone

  • Drag to look, WASD to walk, hold C to crouch (she reads it as an invitation).
  • Click or tap the ground to throw the ball there. Press and rub on Nova to pet her.
  • Keys: 1 sit, 2 down, 3 stay, 4 come, F throw, R drop it, B speak, G good dog, N no.

VR headset

  • Trigger (or pinch with hand tracking) grabs the ball; release while swinging to throw.
  • Touch her head or back with a controller or hand to pet. Squeeze = "good dog".
  • A/X sit, B/Y come, thumbstick click = down.

Phone AR

  • Tap the floor to place her. Then tap her to pet, tap elsewhere to throw. The buttons stay on screen.

Voice

"Nova", "sit", "down", "stay", "come", "fetch", "drop it", "speak", "good girl", "no". Unknown words get a head tilt. Needs a browser with speech recognition and microphone access.

WebXR and the microphone need the page served from its own HTTPS origin. Inside an embedded preview they may be blocked; desktop mode always works.
