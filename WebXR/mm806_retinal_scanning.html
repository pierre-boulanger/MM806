The whole system

Three low-power lasers (red, green, blue) are merged into one beam. A tiny MEMS mirror sweeps it in a raster — fast side-to-side, slow top-to-bottom — and a holographic element on the lens steers every beam through one pinpoint at your pupil. From there the beam lands on the retina and, line by line, paints the image directly onto it.

Representative Japanese system: QD Laser RETISSA retinal-scanning eyewear (uses eye-safe low-power lasers).
