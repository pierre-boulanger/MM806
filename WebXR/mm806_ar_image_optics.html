How the AR image is created

A waveguide combiner. Each display pixel becomes a collimated beam whose angle encodes its position; the lens relays those beams to your eye, which turns angle back into a sharp virtual image.

The whole chain

Light from a tiny micro-display is collimated so each pixel leaves as a parallel beam at its own angle. An in-coupler bends the beams into the lens, where they bounce by total internal reflection. An out-coupler in front of your eye ejects them — copied across an eyebox — and your eye focuses each angle to a point, forming a crisp virtual image that appears to float in the world.

⚠ Angle below the critical angle — light escapes the waveguide instead of guiding (no image).
