Why it needs optics at all

A contact-lens display sits a millimetre or two from your cornea — far inside your near point, so your eye cannot focus it and it would be a useless blur. A tiny lens on the contact (a "femtoprojector") collimates the light from each pixel into a parallel beam. Your eye then focuses those beams onto the retina, exactly as it focuses a distant object — a sharp image at a comfortable apparent distance.
