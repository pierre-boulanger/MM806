Room: "a 6 by 4.5 m room", "5x4 office", "ceiling 3 m".

Add: any item name or synonym from the database, with a count (a, two, 6, a pair of), colour or wood (grey, navy, oak, walnut…) and size words that pick variants (small, large, round, queen, single, 6-seat, "for six").

Where: walls (north/south/east/west, or back/front/left/right as seen from the start view), corners ("in the north-east corner", "in a corner"), "in the centre", and relations to other items: in front of, behind, next to / beside, left of / right of, near / by, on / on top of, above, under, around, facing, opposite / across from, between A and B, on each side of, at the foot of, at the end of. "it" and "them" refer to the previous item. "a lamp on each nightstand" places one per nightstand.

Edit: "move the desk to the east wall", "put the plant in the south-west corner", "rotate the armchair", "turn the armchair to face the TV", "make the sofa blue", "remove the lamp", "remove all chairs", "clear the room".

Mouse/keys: drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Click an item to select it, drag it to move. R rotate, Del delete, Esc deselect, Ctrl+Z undo.

The database lives in this browser (localStorage) and can be edited, imported or exported as JSON. CSV import expects a header row with id,name,syn,w,h,d,color,mount,shape (syn separated by |).
